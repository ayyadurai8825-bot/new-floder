About Iyerpadi Estate

Iyerpadi Estate is known for broad tea plantations, cool mountain air and scenic roads in the Valparai region. The estate reflects the area's plantation heritage and working tea culture.

Estate Viewpoints

The surrounding roads and approved public viewpoints offer views across layered tea slopes, forested ridges and mist-covered valleys.

Visitor Tips

Private estate roads and factory areas may require permission. Visitors should keep to public routes and avoid disturbing workers or tea bushes.

Estate Facts
  • Location: Iyerpadi, Valparai
  • Main crop: Tea
  • Best season: September to March
