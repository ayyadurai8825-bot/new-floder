Aliyar Dam

A scenic reservoir on the route to Valparai

Attraction Details
  • Location: Near Pollachi
  • Known for: Lake and gardens
  • Best for: Picnics and photography

About Aliyar Dam

Aliyar Dam lies along the route to Valparai and is known for its lake, gardens and mountain views. It is a popular stop for picnics and photographs.

Travel Tips

Visit in daylight, follow safety signs and avoid swimming outside designated areas.
