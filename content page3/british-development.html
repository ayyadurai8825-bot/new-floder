Estate Development

British planters developed organised tea and coffee estates during the nineteenth century. The cool climate, heavy rainfall and hilly land made the Anamalai region suitable for plantation crops.

Roads and Settlements

Access roads were constructed to transport workers and plantation materials. Bungalows, factories, schools and small communities gradually developed around estates.

Colonial Legacy

The plantation economy established during British rule remains central to Valparai. Estate names, road layouts, factories and social structures continue to influence the town today.

Quick Facts
  • Era: 19th century onward
  • Main crops: Tea and coffee
  • Infrastructure: Roads and factories
