Chinnakallar

A misty, rain-rich forest landscape

Attraction Details
  • Location: Valparai region
  • Known for: Heavy rainfall
  • Best for: Forest scenery

About Chinnakallar

Chinnakallar receives heavy rainfall and is known for dense vegetation and misty scenery. It is a quiet stop for visitors interested in the region's wetter forest ecosystems.

Travel Tips

Carry rainwear and drive slowly on wet roads. Respect forest restrictions and wildlife areas.
