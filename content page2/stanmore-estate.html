About Stanmore Estate

Stanmore Estate is one of the notable tea estates around Valparai. Its plantation fields, cool mountain climate and mist-covered surroundings reflect the area's long tradition of tea cultivation.

Estate Landscape

Neatly planted tea bushes spread across rolling slopes and form attractive green patterns. The estate setting also offers views of nearby forested hills and changing mountain weather.

Visitor Experience

Visitors can appreciate estate scenery from public viewpoints and nearby roads. Private plantation areas require permission, and guests should avoid entering work zones or blocking vehicles.

Estate Facts
  • Location: Valparai region
  • Main crop: Tea
  • Best feature: Mountain scenery
