About Manambolly Estate

Manambolly Estate is one of the picturesque tea plantation areas around Valparai. It highlights the relationship between mountain agriculture, forest surroundings and local communities.

Estate Scenery

Manambolly offers broad green slopes and changing views influenced by clouds, rainfall and light. It is well suited to slow sightseeing from approved public areas.

Visitor Tips

Respect the estate boundary, seek permission when needed and do not enter harvesting or processing areas. Travel carefully on narrow plantation roads.

Estate Facts
  • Location: Valparai region
  • Main crop: Tea
  • Best feature: Quiet mountain scenery
