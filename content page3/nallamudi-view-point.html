Nallamudi View Point

Panoramic valleys and forested mountain views

Attraction Details
  • Location: Near Valparai estates
  • Known for: Deep valley views
  • Best time: Early morning

About Nallamudi View Point

Nallamudi Poonjolai is a famous viewpoint near tea estates. From here, visitors can see deep valleys, forested slopes and distant dams on clear days.

Travel Tips

Carry a light jacket, stay behind safety barriers and seek estate permission if required.
