About Peria Karamalai Estate

Peria Karamalai Estate forms part of the historic tea plantation network around Valparai. Long green slopes and the cool climate create a calm countryside setting.

Environment and Landscape

The estate combines cultivated tea fields with nearby forested areas and mountain weather. Mists may appear in the morning or during the monsoon season.

Visitor Tips

Observe signs, seek permission for private areas and remain on recognised roads. Carry rain protection and avoid stopping on narrow plantation routes.

Estate Facts
  • Location: Valparai region
  • Main crop: Tea
  • Landscape: Rolling plantations
