Welcome to Valparai

Valparai is a charming hill station in the Anamalai Hills of Tamil Nadu. Surrounded by dense forests, tea and coffee plantations, waterfalls and wildlife, it offers an unforgettable escape from busy city life.

About Valparai

Located about 1,000 metres above sea level, the town enjoys cool weather throughout much of the year. The journey to Valparai passes through scenic roads, forest areas and nearly forty hairpin bends.

Visitors can enjoy estate walks, wildlife sightings, panoramic viewpoints and serene dams. Responsible travel helps protect the region's rich biodiversity and local communities.

Pleasant and cool throughout the year. Carry a light jacket and rain protection during the monsoon season.
